Riley's Decision: Setting Up Her Account

Riley just started her first job at Sunrise Coffee. Her manager hands her a form: direct deposit or paper check, and whether to set up autopay for her phone bill. Riley picks direct deposit and turns on autopay. She also turns on a transaction alert set to fire if her balance drops below $20.

Two months later: her balance is $30. She buys a $12 coffee on the way to work. The purchase goes through — then, a few minutes later, the alert fires: balance now $18. Riley hasn't opted into overdraft coverage, so if she'd tried to spend more than she had, the purchase would have simply been declined instead of going through with a fee.
Explain what happened with Riley's alert, and what she should do differently going forward to avoid this surprise.
